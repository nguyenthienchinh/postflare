    How it works

    From idea to published in three steps

    No browser extensions, no scraping, no shared passwords. Postflare uses each platform's official sign-in and publishing APIs; the one unofficial integration (Skool) is experimental and off by default.

    1. 1

      Connect your accounts

      Sign in to each network with its official OAuth screen (Google, TikTok, Meta, X, LinkedIn and others). You choose which accounts Postflare may post to, and you can revoke access at any time.

    2. 2

      Compose and schedule

      Write once, attach media from your library, adjust the post for each network and pick a time on the calendar. Or ask your AI assistant to do it over MCP.

    3. 3

      Postflare publishes on time

      At the scheduled moment a Cloudflare Workflow publishes the post through the platform's official API, retries temporary failures and records the result and the live link.

    Privacy and data use

    Your accounts, your data, your infrastructure

    Postflare is self-hosted. Each deployment runs in its owner's own Cloudflare account; there is no central Postflare server that receives your tokens or content.

    Google and YouTube data use

    • Postflare uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos that the signed-in user chooses to their own YouTube channel, and to set each video's title, description, privacy status, playlists and thumbnail, and to post a first comment on it.
    • OAuth tokens are encrypted and stored only in the owner's own Cloudflare account (D1 database). They are used for nothing other than publishing the user's scheduled posts.
    • No Google user data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
    • You can disconnect your channel in Postflare at any time, which deletes the stored tokens, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

    Postflare's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

    TikTok

    Postflare uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API only to show your basic profile and to publish the videos and photos you schedule to your own TikTok account, with the privacy and interaction settings you choose. Access can be revoked in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

    Meta: Facebook, Instagram, Threads

    Postflare uses Meta's official Graph APIs only to publish posts, reels and stories to the Pages and professional accounts you connect. Remove access any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations, or Instagram Settings → Apps and websites.

    Self-hosted and open source

    Runs on your Cloudflare account. Free.

    Postflare is AGPL-3.0 licensed and fits comfortably in the Cloudflare Workers Free plan. One click deploys the Worker, database, storage bucket and workflow to your account; your tokens and media never leave it.

    Inspired by Postiz, the open-source social media scheduler, and rebuilt to be Cloudflare-native: no servers, no database to run. Postflare is an independent project, not affiliated with Postiz.
